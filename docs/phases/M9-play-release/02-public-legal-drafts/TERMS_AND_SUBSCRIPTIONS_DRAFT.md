## 1. Parties and eligible use

These terms would govern the relationship between **[LEGAL PUBLISHER]** and a parent/guardian using Madalasa–Alarka. The user represents that they have the legal authority to supervise and configure the child's device in **[SUPPORTED TERRITORIES]**. The service is for appropriately authorized parental monitoring, not covert tracking of spouses, adults or third parties.

## 2. Service description

The planned services include some combination of location history, app usage reports, authenticated controls on supported devices, and optionally authorized storage integrations. Available features vary by Android version, handset, permissions, subscription status and device-management enrollment. No uninterrupted location or universal restriction/camera/audio capability is guaranteed.

The parent app and child app may be separate applications. Managed-device provisioning must be described before enrollment, including any need to reset the phone and the effect on device use.

## 3. Transparency and lawful use

The child-side app shall remain identifiable and present Android- and Play-required monitoring notifications and disclosures. Use of the service to bypass consent requirements or Android security protections is prohibited. Customers may not use the app for unauthorized surveillance.

## 4. Account and security

Parent accounts must be protected by appropriate credentials. The parent is responsible for access they authorize, subject to applicable consumer law. Account recovery, compromised credential reporting and support: **[SUPPORT EMAIL / WEB URL]**.

## 5. Paid plans

## 7. Service availability and limitations

Location/network/device behavior may be delayed or unavailable. App-control features may work only after managed enrollment. Remote command delivery is not the same as successful execution. The service is not a substitute for emergency services.

## 8. Suspension and termination
