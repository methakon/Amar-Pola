## Intake categories and actions
1. Data access/correction/erasure request: verify adult guardian subject, role and child-scope; never give precise GPS merely on a claim of parenthood.
2. Wrong-family child exposure/compromised tokens, covert app behavior, active safety issue: immediate restricted security escalation; determine CERT-In reportability and platform notifications.
3. Permission dispute, custody/guardian disagreement, child safety: restrict disputed access without automatically revealing confidential reporter; obtain qualified legal review.
4. Location/usage/media collection complaint: identify exact feature, permission purpose and Android OS status; stop potentially unauthorized collection and preserve minimized evidence.
5. Billing/cancellation/refund: use Play-managed subscription rules when actually launched; route to merchant/support with a case ID.
6. Accessibility/notice/support: provide readable formats and language assistance once staffed.

## Case workflow
Accessible app/web intake → stable ID, privacy-preserving timestamp → identity and authority verification → category/severity → minimum-needed evidence → assigned officer → restricted review → documented determination and remedy → securely notify complainant → appeal/escalation route → deletion/retention per approved schedule. All actions audited and role-bound.

**Avoid child GPS, recording or family identity in general helpdesk tickets, GitHub issues or analytics.** High-risk cases require confidential channel and case-scoped admin access; support agent default is redacted aggregates.
